Here’s an overview of the judges from ZK Hack Montréal, highlighting their backgrounds and contributions to the zero-knowledge (ZK) and cryptography space:

---

### 🧑⚖️ Judges at ZK Hack Montréal

#### **Anna Rose**

Anna is the co-founder of Zero Knowledge Validator and host of the Zero Knowledge Podcast, a leading platform exploring zero-knowledge proofs, cryptography, and privacy. ([IQ.wiki][1])

#### **Zaki Manian**

Zaki is a co-founder of Iqlusion, providing infrastructure for next-generation crypto technologies. ([Blockworks][2])

#### **Nico Mohnblatt**

Nico is an applied cryptographer and researcher focused on zero-knowledge proof systems and privacy-enhancing technology. ([Bain Capital Crypto][3])

#### **Tarun Chitra**

Tarun is the CEO and co-founder of Gauntlet, a simulation platform for crypto networks. ([Clay][4])

#### **David Wong**

David is a co-founder of zkSecurity, author of "Real-World Cryptography," and a research advisor at Archetype. ([LinkedIn][5])

#### **Tracy Livengood**

Tracy is the co-founder of Pluto, an applied cryptography organization building developer tools that integrate verifiable web data into on-chain applications using zero-knowledge proofs. ([ZK PODCAST][6])

#### **Mikerah Quintyne-Collins**

Mikerah is an independent researcher and founder of Stoffel and HashCloak, focusing on blockchain privacy R\&D. ([RootData][7])

#### **Alex Pruden**

Alex is the Executive Director at the Aleo Network Foundation. ([aleo.org][8])

#### **Richard Liang**

Richard is the founder of ZKP2P, a platform leveraging zero-knowledge proofs for peer-to-peer applications. ([Bain Capital Crypto][3])

#### **Henry de Valence**

Henry is the founder of Penumbra, a privacy-preserving proof-of-stake blockchain aiming to unite privacy and decentralized finance (DeFi). ([hdevalence.ca][9])

#### **Ismael Hishon-Rezaizadeh**

Ismael is the founder and CEO of Lagrange Labs, focusing on zero-knowledge data interoperability. ([YouTube][10])

#### **Yuki Yuminaga**

Yuki is a lead researcher at Fenbushi Capital, with interests in MEV, privacy, consensus mechanisms, and trust-minimized middleware. ([WebX][11])

#### **Connor O'Hara**

Connor is a software engineer at Celestia Labs, working on modular blockchain architectures.&#x20;

#### **Wei Dai**

Wei is a research partner at 1kx, focusing on research and investment in blockchain infrastructure and emergent technologies. ([wdai.us][12])

#### **Gaylord Warner**

Gaylord is a member of the ZK Hack team, contributing to the organization and execution of zero-knowledge hackathons. ([zkSummit][13])

---

These individuals are at the forefront of zero-knowledge research and development, contributing to various aspects of cryptography, blockchain infrastructure, and privacy-enhancing technologies.
