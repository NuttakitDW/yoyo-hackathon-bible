## 🧠 ZK Hack Sponsor Breakdown

### 🔷 **o1Labs**

* **What they do:**
  Creator of the [Mina Protocol](https://minaprotocol.com), the world’s lightest blockchain, and **o1js**, a TypeScript library for building zero-knowledge applications.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Build applications using **o1js** (zk-SNARKs with TypeScript DSL).
  * Explore domains like privacy-preserving games, on-chain voting, and identity.
  * Show creativity using o1js circuits, especially if it runs in the browser.

---

### 🟩 **NovaNet**

* **What they do:**
  Decentralized infrastructure for running ZK applications—provides provers, proof generation tools, and middleware for ZK workflows.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Use **NovaNet’s proving infra or SDK** in your app.
  * Push the boundary on zkApps that require offloading heavy computation.
  * Focus on latency-sensitive or compute-heavy ZK use cases (e.g., ML inference, large-scale attestations).

---

### ⚙️ **Gevulot**

* **What they do:**
  A permissionless L1 network for **ZK proving and verification at scale**, allowing any team to deploy custom provers/verifiers like smart contracts.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Deploy novel **ZK proof workloads** on Gevulot (e.g., rollups, zkVMs, bridges).
  * Benchmark and stress test ZK proof generation.
  * Combine Gevulot’s off-chain prover network with on-chain logic.

---

### 🧭 **ZKV (Zero Knowledge Validator)**

* **What they do:**
  Organizer of ZK Hack. Supports ZK ecosystem through education, grants, meetups, and curation.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Strong community presence. Not focused on tech stacks.
  * Reward clarity, originality, and user-oriented zk apps.
  * Bonus if project aligns with ecosystem growth or privacy values.

---

### 🔨 **RISC Zero**

* **What they do:**
  Creates a general-purpose zkVM (based on STARKs) that allows developers to run any Rust program and generate ZK proofs of its execution.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Build apps that prove computation integrity using **RISC Zero zkVM**.
  * Explore use cases like verifiable machine learning, private compute, zk coprocessors, or optimistic bridges.
  * Use guest/host pattern correctly and demonstrate performance awareness.

---

### 🛡 **Polygon (zkEVM / Zero team)**

* **What they do:**
  Research and product arm behind **Polygon zkEVM**, **Plonky2**, and other recursive SNARK systems for L2 scalability.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Build on **Polygon zkEVM** (EVM-compatible ZK rollup).
  * Use **Plonky2** if working on recursive circuits or proving pipelines.
  * Propose realistic dApps (e.g., zk identity, zk bridges) with clear integration value to Polygon's L2s.

---

### 🟦 **Horizen Labs (zkVerify)**

* **What they do:**
  Building **zkVerify**, a ZK proof verification layer for modular blockchain architectures.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Implement circuits that highlight proof **efficiency, modularity, or composability**.
  * Focus on cross-chain proofs, proof aggregation, or modular verifier contracts.

---

### 🧰 **Devfolio**

* **What they do:**
  India’s leading hackathon platform. Logistics and infrastructure partner.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Primarily supports event operations, not technical judging.
  * Encourages high-quality submissions and thorough documentation.

---

### 🌍 **w3.group**

* **What they do:**
  Event co-organizer in Europe (Berlin, Lisbon); runs local ZK communities.
* **Hackathon expectation:**

  * Promote usable apps that can demo locally.
  * Bonus for integration with local tech stack, hardware wallets, or L2s.

---
